A little of home

A familiar surface.
A new gathering place.

The idea began with a house move and a stack of floorboards nobody wanted to leave behind. The brief was simple: make something that could hold onto a little of that home, and still work in a smaller dining room.

The design keeps a generous, uncomplicated top, with space for an extra chair when friends drop in. Old fixing marks become part of the surface; the edges are softened where hands naturally rest.

The details you live with.

A finish should feel good under a forearm and forgive the occasional spill. The imagined specification pairs a natural-looking surface with an easy care routine, and joinery designed to let the top move with the seasons.
