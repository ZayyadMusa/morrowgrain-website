Your details

A little privacy.
A clear explanation.

Here’s what this website does with the information you choose to share.

When you prepare an enquiry

The enquiry form keeps your details in the current page’s memory while you review them. Preparing an enquiry does not send it anywhere. Closing or reloading the page clears the form’s working copy, although your browser may offer its own autofill.

When you save or copy it

“Save enquiry” creates a text file on your device. “Copy enquiry” copies the text to your clipboard, when your browser permits it. You decide where to share that copy.

When email or delivery is available

If an email address has been connected to this website, “Open email app” prepares a message in your email app. You still choose whether to send it. If direct form delivery has been connected, “Send enquiry” transmits your name, email address, location, idea and selected options to the workshop’s configured form service. A sent message appears only after that service accepts the request.

A small preference

The “Reduce motion” choice may be saved in your browser’s local storage so the site can remember it. It contains only an on/off preference, not your enquiry or personal details. Your device’s reduced-motion setting is also respected. The website does not add analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Local media

The film, photographs and fonts are served with the website. The page does not contact a stock media service or font provider when you browse. Hosting providers may process standard technical request information to serve the site.

Questions about your information

Use the workshop contact details provided with your commission for requests about information you have sent. Before using this website for a real business, the owner should add their identity, contact details, actual form service and retention arrangements here.
