The brief was a light, open set of shelves for a corner beside a window. The available boards varied in width and color, so the design needed to let those differences feel intentional.

We imagine a simple timber frame with shelves sized around the useful lengths. The rhythm follows the room and the objects it holds, leaving enough open space for daylight to pass through.

Enough room to breathe.

The finish takes its cue from the light in the room. A calm surface keeps the grain visible, and secure fixings are planned around the actual wall, shelf span and intended load.
